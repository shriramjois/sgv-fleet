Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on fares, outstation minimums, toll charges, and driver allowances.

Our standard quotes include vehicle rental, fuel, and chauffeur charges. Fastag toll charges, parking tickets at tourist destinations, and interstate border entry taxes (if crossing into Kerala, Tamil Nadu, or Andhra Pradesh) are paid as per actuals by the guest during the trip or billed on final invoice.
For all outstation round trips originating from Bangalore or Mangalore, a standard minimum of 300 km per calendar day applies. If the total kilometers driven exceed this cumulative average (e.g. 600 km for 2 days), extra kilometers are charged at the vehicle's standard per-km rate.
Driver allowance (Batta) is charged on a per-day basis (₹400 for Etios, ₹500 for Crysta/Rumion, ₹600 for Tempo Traveller, and ₹800 for Urbania). If the trip involves night driving between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, a standard nominal night allowance applies.
Yes, 100%. Driving the Western Ghat hairpins (Charmadi, Shiradi, Sampaje, or Bisle Ghat) requires seasoned commercial drivers. All our drivers operating between Mangalore, Udupi, and Bangalore have over 7+ years of ghat handling experience, strictly observing hill-station safety norms.
Yes. We issue formal GST tax invoices for all corporate transfers, conference delegations, and business guest travel across Bangalore and Mangalore. Simply share your organization's GSTIN and billing address during booking.
