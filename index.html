Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on fares, outstation minimums, toll charges, and driver allowances.

Our standard quotes include vehicle rental, fuel, and chauffeur charges. Fastag toll charges, parking tickets at tourist destinations, and interstate border entry taxes (if crossing into Kerala, Tamil Nadu, or Andhra Pradesh) are paid as per actuals by the guest during the trip or billed on final invoice.
For all outstation round trips originating from Bangalore or Mangalore, a standard minimum of 250 km to 300 km per calendar day applies (depending on the vehicle class). If the total kilometers driven exceed this cumulative average, extra kilometers are charged at the vehicle's standard per-km rate.
Driver allowance (Batta) is charged on a per-day basis (typically ₹350–₹700/day depending on vehicle class). If the trip involves night driving between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, a standard nominal night allowance is applicable to ensure driver safety and refreshment.
